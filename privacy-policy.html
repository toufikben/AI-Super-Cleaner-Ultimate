AI Super Cleaner Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 13, 2026

AI Super Cleaner is developed by Toufik Bendjeddah. This policy explains how the Android application handles information when you use its cleaning, storage-management, privacy-scanning, security, advertising, and in-app purchase features.

Information processed by the app

The app processes files, folders, media metadata, storage information, installed-app information, and Wi-Fi information on your device when you request a scan or related feature. This processing is performed locally on the device to identify junk files, duplicates, large files, privacy risks, storage usage, and network-security information. The app does not upload your personal files, vault contents, scan results, or installed-app list to the developer.

Vault files and PIN-related data are stored locally using Android security mechanisms. Preferences, scan history, cleanup rules, and local application state are stored on the device.

Permissions

The app requests storage and media permissions to scan and manage files. It requests MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE for its full-file cleaning and management functions on supported Android versions. It requests QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES because Privacy Scanner examines installed applications and their declared permissions. It may request Wi-Fi and Android-version-specific location permissions for Wi-Fi security information, notifications for alerts, biometric permissions for the private vault, and boot or battery-related permissions for scheduled functionality.

Advertising and consent

The free version uses Google AdMob interstitial and rewarded advertisements. The Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's consent platform may process information such as advertising identifiers, device and app information, approximate location inferred by the provider, diagnostics, and ad-interaction information according to Google's policies and your consent choices. The app requests consent through Google's User Messaging Platform where required. Premium users do not receive the app's advertisements.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In-app purchases

The app uses Google Play Billing for the premium_monthly subscription and premium_lifetime one-time purchase. Google Play processes payment, purchase status, renewals, cancellations, and refunds. The developer does not receive your payment-card details.

Sharing

The developer does not sell or share your files, vault contents, scan results, or installed-app list. Information processed by Google AdMob, Google UMP, and Google Play Billing is handled by those services under their own policies and settings.

Children

The app is a general-purpose utility. It is not directed specifically to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Data retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until you delete it through the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Ad and billing providers retain information according to their own policies and account controls.

Security

The app uses Android platform storage and security APIs and avoids cleartext network traffic. No security measure can guarantee absolute protection, so keep your device and Google account secure.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or applicable requirements change. Questions about this policy can be sent to www.toufik.bendjeddah@gmail.com.
